Section 26-78. Exceptions to Prohibition.

This Article does not prohibit:

A.
 The receipt or use by the City of ALPR Data that is produced to the City pursuant to a search warrant, a court order, or a subpoena issued in a specific case.

B.
 The receipt or use by the city of ALPR Data provided to the City by a court of competent jurisdiction or a defendant in a criminal matter or offered to the City by a member of the public, a private entity, or a federal, state, county, or municipal agency in connection with a specific investigation or offense, provided that no City agency specifically requested or directed the underlying Query of any ALPR Data.

C.
 Conduct that the City or its officers are required or expressly authorized to undertake by federal or state law, judicial warrant, or lawful order from a court of competent jurisdiction.

D.
 The use by a City Agency of an ALPR System or ALPR Data for business purposes unrelated to law enforcement such as parking lot or garage enforcement and management; provided that such use of an ALPR System or ALPR Data does not cause any person's location, movements, personally identifying information, or other information in which a member of the public might have a reasonable expectation of privacy to be made available to or accessible by any private third party for collection and inclusion in any dataset which may be provided to any public or private third party as a searchable database by the collecting third party.

E.
 The receipt or review and analysis of ALPR Data by a law enforcement officer participating in a multi-jurisdictional task force or multi-jurisdictional investigation when such ALPR Data was lawfully obtained by another law enforcement agency, provided that no City agency specifically requested or directed the underlying Query of any ALPR Data.
